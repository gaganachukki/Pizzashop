01 — How It Began

Born From a Pure Love for Authentic Crust

STACKLY started with a single wood-fired oven and a simple conviction: fast food should never mean compromised food. We believed pizza was more than cheese on dough; it was a celebratory centerpiece that brings family and friends closer.

After traveling through the historic pizzerias of Naples and collaborating with master bakers, we developed our signature sourdough recipe. Today, STACKLY brings that same artisan soul into every single box delivered straight to your door.

06 — The Artisanal Method

Mastering the Pizza Craft

From flour milling to blazing wood-fired oven heat, take a behind-the-scenes look at how we bake perfection.

01

48-Hour Slow Fermentation

Natural levain yeast dough rests in temperature-controlled chillers for two full days, developing complex aromas and a light, digestible crumb.

02

Hand-Stretched Pizzaiolo Technique

No rolling pins ever touch our dough. Our chefs gently stretch by hand to preserve delicate air pockets that create a bubbly, airy cornicione crust.

03

High-Heat Blistered Baking

Baked at scorching 450°C stone temperatures for 90 to 120 seconds, sealing moisture inside while blistering the crust with leopard spots.
