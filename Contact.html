08 — Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our dough, delivery times, payment methods, and gluten-free options.

How does STACKLY make its pizza dough so light and digestible?

We use a proprietary 48-hour cold fermentation process. By allowing our natural levain sourdough to mature slowly in chilled proofing cabinets, the starches and complex proteins break down naturally. This yields an airy, blistered crust that never leaves you feeling heavy.

What is the average delivery time?

Our orders are dispatched in custom thermal-insulated delivery pods within 10 minutes of coming out of our 450°C wood stone oven. Average delivery time is 25 to 35 minutes depending on your distance from our nearest kitchen hub.

Do you offer 100% vegetarian options?

Yes! Over half of our menu is 100% pure vegetarian, baked using dedicated utensils and separate preparation surfaces to prevent any cross-contamination.

Can I customize my pizza crust, sauces, and toppings?

Absolutely! Use our interactive "Build Your Own Pizza" feature available directly on our Homepage and Menu pages to choose your crust, cheese blend, artisan sauces, and unlimited toppings.

How does this frontend demonstration work?

This entire STACKLY website runs entirely in your browser using modern HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, GSAP, and LocalStorage. You can place demonstration orders, customize pizzas, add coupons, and manage orders across the Admin and Client dashboards in real time without any backend server.
