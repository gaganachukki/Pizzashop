Deconstructing Culinary Art

Anatomy of a Neapolitan Slice

Every single layer of a STACKLY pizza is mathematically engineered for absolute equilibrium between crunch, acidity, richness, and floral aroma.

01
The Crust Rim

The Leopard Cornicione

High-hydration sourdough dough creates airy, hollow micro-chambers that blister under 450°C heat, giving a crisp wafer shell with a marshmallow-light interior.

02
San Marzano D.O.P.

Volcanic Tomato Coulis

Hand-crushed whole plum tomatoes grown in volcanic soils near Mount Vesuvius, preserved with zero sugar and balanced strictly with mineral-rich Trapani sea salt.

03
Artisanal Curd

Fior di Latte & Mozzarella

Whole-milk curd pulled daily into silky braided knots with 52% moisture that melt into glossy white islands without drowning the crust in grease.

04
Finishing Drizzle

Ligurian EVOO & Sweet Basil

Cold-pressed extra virgin olive oil with peppery finish notes and freshly picked sweet Genovese basil leaves added at the oven mouth to release volatile aromatics.
