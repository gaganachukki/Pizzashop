Cold Sourdough Proofing

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Service Questions

Find quick answers regarding our catering booking, delivery radiuses, allergens, and corporate billing.

How does STACKLY guarantee 30-minute delivery during peak rush hours?
We operate strategically positioned micro-kitchen hubs across the city. Orders are dispatched to the closest hub immediately. Because our wood-fired ovens bake at 450°C in just 90 seconds, pizzas are baked, boxed, and on their way within 6 minutes of placing the order, leaving 24 minutes for localized delivery.
How far in advance should I book party or corporate catering?
For Party Pods and Corporate Feasts (up to 35 guests), a 4-hour advance heads-up is sufficient. For our Grand Artisanal Live Banquet featuring our on-site stone oven and dedicated chefs, we suggest booking at least 48 hours in advance to guarantee date and staff reservation.
Can pizzas be customized for vegan, gluten-friendly, or halal preferences?
Yes! We offer 100% plant-based mozzarella, gluten-friendly millet dough blends, and clearly designated vegetarian and non-vegetarian preparation stations with separate cutters and ovens to avoid cross-contamination.
Do you offer corporate credit terms and GST tax invoicing?
Yes, corporate clients can establish corporate billing accounts with centralized monthly invoicing, designated PO tracking, GST-compliant tax invoices, and recurring weekly team lunch scheduling.
